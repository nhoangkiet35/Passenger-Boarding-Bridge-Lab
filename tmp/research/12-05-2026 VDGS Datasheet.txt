FEATURES
The Honeywell Navitas
TM
 Gate and Turnaround 
Solution introduces the latest Honeywell 
Navitas® Smart Visual Docking which enables 
automated, precise and safe docking.
The solution is based on multi-sensor data fusion with advanced sensor technologies 
such as long-range, high-resolution 3D Digital LIDAR and High Dynamic Range 
CMOS* (HDRC) video sensor. This provides a smooth, seamless docking experience, 
even in airports with complex gate layouts and low-visibility conditions.
Honeywell Navitas® Smart Visual Docking is fully compliant with International 
Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) requirements: ICAO Annex 14 Aerodromes 
Vol. I, 8th Edition, 2018 Chapter 5.3.26, and Aerodrome Design Manual 
Document (Doc 9157-AN/901), Part 4, Visual Aids. The conformity to EN 
50512:2009 is affirmed. Thereby the prerequisites for operation according 
EASA Certification Specifications and Guidance Material for Aerodromes Design 
CS-ADR-DSN, Issue 3 from December 08th, 2016, Book 1, Chapter M, CS-ADR-
DSN.M.760 and Book 2, Chapter M, GM1 ADR-DSN.M.760 are fulfilled.
