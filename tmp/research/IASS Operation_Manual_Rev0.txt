1. INTRODUCTION 
Implementation of a bridge monitoring system consists of 32 bridges with WAGO 
SCADA to transform maintenance from reactive to proactive, enhances safety, and 
supports data-driven asset management. By combining sensor networks, robust data 
acquisition, real-time visualization, and 3D presentation, users gain continuous insight 
into structural behavior under variable loads and environmental conditions. Adhering 
to best practices in sensor deployment, data management, cybersecurity, and 
stakeholder coordinatio n ensures the monitoring system delivers reliable early 
warnings, optimizes maintenance, and ultimately prolongs the service life of critical 
bridge infrastructure. 
 
1.1 OBJECTIVE 
The objective of implementing WAGO SCADA solution is to establish a robust, secure, 
and scalable system that enables real-time monitoring, control, and data-driven decision-
making for industrial or infrastructure processes. Specific goals include: 
 
1. Ensure Continuous Real-Time Visibility 
- Deploy controllers and distributed I/O to acquire critical process or structural 
parameters (e.g., temperature, pressure, flow, vibration). 
- Provide operators and engineers with dashboards that display  3D modelling, 
live status, trend charts, and key performance indicators. 
 
2. Implement Effective Alarm Management 
- Collaborate with user to define meaningful alarms. Assign severity levels (e.g., 
critical, high, medium) based on risk and potential impact.  
- Continuously log all alarm events (activations /clearances) with timestamps. 
Store historical data in a historian or database for analysis 
- Implement Shelving mechanisms  technique which  allow operators to 
temporarily suppress low -priority alarms during certain operations  to avoid 
nuisance alarms. 
 
3. Support Live Data Trend Logging and Historical Trend Analysis 
- Captures and visualizes process parameters in real time, enabling users to 
observe ongoing behavior and detect deviations quickly. 
- Store timestamped tag values , then summaries and display in term of hourly, 
daily, monthly or yearly depending on the database.  
 
4. Facilitate Integration  
- Utilize for standard industrial protocols (e.g., Modbus TCP/RTU) 
- Ensure seamless data exchange for reporting
