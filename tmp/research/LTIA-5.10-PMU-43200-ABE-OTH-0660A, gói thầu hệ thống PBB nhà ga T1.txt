Additional information 
 
The Training Course is designed to equip maintenance staff of ShinMaywa products with the knowledge and skills required to diagnose and 
fix electrical/electronic issues effectively. To participate in this course, you should meet the following qualifications: 
 
 
 
• Basic Understanding of Mechanical/Electrical/Electronic Principles: Participants should have a basic understanding of 
electrical/electronic principles, including knowledge of electrical/electronic circuits, components, and safety precautions. This 
knowledge will help participants to comprehend and apply the concepts taught during the training. 
 
• English Proficiency: The training will be conducted in English, and participants should be proficient in English to understand and 
communicate effectively. 
 
• Relevant Work Experience: Participants should have relevant work experience in the maintenance of electrical/electronic 
equipment. This experience should include electrical/electronic troubleshooting, repair, and maintenance of ShinMaywa 
products or similar equipment. 
 
• Safety Awareness: Participants should be aware of the importance of safety in the workplace, particularly when working with 
electrical/electronic equipment. A safety-conscious attitude and adherence to safety procedures are essential for successful 
completion of the course. 
 
• Commitment to Learning: The course requires active participation in lectures, demonstrations, and hands-on training sessions. 
 
• Certificate of competency: Participants will receive a Course Completion Certificate after successful completion of the course.

Fundamental Safety Precautions 
General items 
 
Make sure to observe the following items to assure safe operation. 
 
1. Read the instruction manual. 
 
• Incorrect operating procedures can be extremely dangerous, and cause a fatal accident involving loss of life or 
accident resulting in injury or death. 
Make sure to fully understand this manual before operating the boarding bridge. 
• Always store this instruction manual in a specified place so that every person handling the boarding bridge can 
read it repeatedly and thoroughly understand the boarding bridge. 
 
2. Wear safe clothes! 
• Wear neat clothes appropriate to actions. 
Make sure to wear protective devices suitable for operation. 
 
 
3. Never operate the boarding bridge while you are in poor physical conditions or under the influence of alcohol or 
medicine. 
• You may be slow in judgment or give wrong judgment while you are in poor physical condition.  Never operate 
the boarding bridge in such condition. 
Never operate the boarding bridge while you are under the influence of alcohol or medicine or using an audio 
player or mobile phone. 
Wear proper, fitting clothes. 
Otherwise, your clothes may be caught by the joystick lever, etc., and cause an accident 
resulting in injury or death or property damage. 
CAUTION

4. Inspect the boarding bridge. 
 
⦿ If you find any abnormality, immediately contact a maintenance company. 
• Move the boarding bridge to the standby place or a place where the boarding bridge does not hinder operations 
of aircraft. 
 
⦿ Inspection and maintenance 
• Perform “daily care” and “daily inspection and maintenance” by yourself as instructed in this manual. 
Store records of “daily care” and “daily inspection and maintenance” that will be important information in 
“specialized periodical inspection and maintenance”. 
• “Specialized periodical inspection and maintenance” require specialized knowledge. 
Ask our company or a specialized maintenance company. Because “specialized periodical inspection and 
maintenance” require some time, it is recommended to stop spot operations.  
 
 
5. In case of an action against accident or fire 
• The customer is to determine actions to be taken against an accident and fire. 
• When a property damage accident occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, report the accident 
situation to our company, and follow our instructions. 
• When an accident resulting in injury or death occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, and give 
proper treatment to the injured. 
• When fire disaster occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, and evacuate people from the 
boarding bridge to a safe place. 
• Determine evacuation sites and locations of fire extinguisher to assure safety. It is recommended to 
periodically hold an evacuation drill. 
 
6. Action against fire disaster caused by earthquake, lighting stroke, typhoon, etc. 
• When an earthquake occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge. When the earthquake intensity is 
4 or more (Japanese scale), check for abnormalities. 
• When the boarding bridge is hit by a lightning stroke, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, and 
check for abnormalities caused by the lightning stroke. 
• In the case of typhoon, the operating procedures are regulated by the wind velocity. Refer to the specification 
list. 
 
“Specialized periodical inspection and maintenance” require specialized knowledge. 
A maintenance company is to perform them with its responsibility. 
A maintenance company is a company which works under contract for performing inspection and 
maintenance. 
Our company shall not be responsible for failures, breakages and accidents caused by inspection 
and/or maintenance performed by any company other than our company. 
IMPORTANT 
If there are any abnormalities, contact your maintenance company.

Cautions on Operating Procedures 
 
1. Maintain a correct posture during operation! 
 
 
2. Avoid concurrent works. 
• Do not move up or down the closure curtain while moving the boarding bridge. 
• Do not open or close the cab roller shutter while moving the boarding bridge. 
 
3. Prevent contact. 
• Pay strict attention to avoid contact of the boarding bridge to an aircraft. 
Note that the landing frame comes near the aircraft engine when the service stair is installed on the right side of 
the boarding bridge. 
 
 
Cautions after Operating Procedures 
 
1. Store the boarding bridge in a specified place! 
• Return the boarding bridge to a specified standby place. 
 
2. Turn OFF the power of the control console before leaving the boarding bridge! 
• Make sure to set the control key switch to “OFF”, pull out the key, and store the key in a specified place.

Attention Items 
A Warning Nameplates 
All warning nameplates adhered on the boarding bridge and their positions warn passengers or PBB personnel to follow 
instructions specified. 
Warning nameplates indicate the contents related to operating procedures. 
Check the contents once again and operate the boarding bridge safely. 
Warning nameplates should be always adhered on the boarding bridge to assure safety of all workers concerned with the 
boarding bridge. If warning nameplates are lost or damaged, immediately contact ShinMaywa.  
 
 
 
B Warning Beacons, Obstruction Lights and Floodlights 
Two (2) orange rotation beacons fixed under the PBB, which warn anyone, near the PBB in operation. 
Two (2) obstruction lights on top of the cab are constantly illuminated and visible from a distance.  
Two (2) floodlight lights under the cab floor used to illuminate the aircraft fuselage and around PBB drive wheels. 
 
C Warning Horn 
 
One (1) electric horn (audible siren) is installed under the cab floor to warn that the PBB is in motion.

1. Basic Operating Procedures 
 
This section explains the basic operating procedures. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1) When the [EMERGENCY STOP] pushbutton switch ① on the control console is pressed, press it again to 
restore the boarding bridge from the emergency stop status. 
 
① 
Confirm before operation that there is no person inside the boarding bridge. 
Confirm that there is no person, vehicle, etc. within the operation range of the boarding bridge.

- The safety hoop does not prevent damage, but reduces damage. 
Do not put your foot on or ride on the safety hoop. it may fall or damage the safety hoop,

Caution 
When the sensor detects an obstacle such as a person, the boarding bridge will stop, but depending on the traveling 
speed, inertia may work and it may not stop immediately. 
- The safety hoop does not prevent damage, but reduces damage. 
 Depending on the surface condition of the apron and tires, the stopping distance will be approximately 150 mm or 
less. Therefore, when the stop tape switch is detected, if the distance between the object to be detected and the road 
surface contact part of the tire is within 150 mm, it may get caught in the tire. In addition, detection may be delayed 
due to the shape of the object to be detected or the escape in the opposite direction when contacting.  
Although it is expected to reduce the damage caused by serious accidents due to entanglement, there is a possibility of 
bruising due to contact with the bumper (parts on the bumper side may also be damaged).  
This device is a safety device. In daily inspection, press the tape switch with your finger etc. to check if obstacle 
detection works normally. 
During inspection, check that there are no abnormalities in the tape switch (damage such as cracks or scratches on the 
rubber part), wiring (scratch, abnormal slack), bending of the mechanical part, or fastening part.  
The safety standard for this device is 5 years for electronic devices such as tape switches, wiring, and remote I / O. 
 When changing tires, remove the bolts attached to the safety hoop (8 on each side, 16 in total) and remove the guard

1 Daily Care (Cleaning) 
It is recommended to clean the inside and outside of the boarding bridge periodically for comfortable use. 
 
 
 
(1) Drain mechanism 
Do not let any blockage gather in the gutters (drain ditches), rainwater gutters and drain pipes under the tunnel, or 
let the drain mechanism be clogged. Remove snow, ice, etc. from the drain pipes under the tunnel and rainwater 
gutters if there are any. 
 
(2) Control console and control panel 
Wipe dirt off the control console and control panel with dry cloth. 
 
(3) Walls and ceiling 
Wipe dirt off the walls and ceiling with diluted neutral detergent. 
 
(4) Floor 
Remove dusts from the floor using a vacuum cleaner. Wipe the floor if it is wet. 
Contact your maintenance company when the floor is considerably dirty. 
 
(5) Windows 
Polish glass windows in the cab, tunnel and service door with commercially-available cleaner. 
 
(6) Double-glazed glass … In the glass type boarding bridge if applicable 
Polish the inner and outer surfaces of double-glazed glass with commercially-available cleaner. 
When dirt is not easily removed, put it with diluted neutral detergent and wipe it off.  Wash the glass with water 
immediately after that, and then finish the glass with clean cloth. 
Clean the glass approximately once in 3 months. 
 
(7) Removal of snow and ice (in winter if applicable) 
Remove snow from the boarding bridge roof. 
Melt ice adhered on the moving parts and cab floor.

6 Structure of Each Part 
This section explains the structure of each part of the boarding bridge. 
 
6-1 Rotunda … Refer to Fig. 2-13. 
The rotunda connects the terminal building to the tunnel section A, and the boarding bridge turns around the 
rotunda during operation. 
The floor, ceiling and corridor remain stationary even during operation. 
The rigid frame and roof turn around the rotunda column. 
With regard to the left and right rotunda curtains, one is wound by the barrel and the other is unwound from the  
barrel as the boarding bridge turns. 
The curtain always holds proper tension due to springs incorporated in the barrel. 
The curtain guide roller and vertical roller work as the guides, and let the curtain fit along the ceiling and floor. 
 
6-2 Rotunda column … Refer to Fig. 2-13. 
The rotunda column supports the rotunda and works as the rotation axis while the rotunda turns during operation. 
 
6-3 Tunnel … Refer to Figs. 2-14-1 and 2-14-2. 
In the 2-section telescoping type, the tunnel consists of the tunnel sections A and B (from the rotunda side).  In 
the 3-section telescoping type, the tunnel consists of the tunnel sections A, B and C (from the rotunda side). 
The boarding bridge extends and retracts among the tunnel sections A, B and C. 
Several types of rollers are provided among the extending/retracting tunnel sections. 
The tunnel is made of corrugated steel plates and steel plates. In the glass type, the tunnel side is made of glass. 
 
(1) Rails and rollers … Figs. 2-14-1 and 2-14-2 
Rollers are provided among the extending/retracting tunnel sections so that the boarding bridge smoothly extends 
and retracts. The upper portion of the tunnel works as a rail for roller movement.

6-5 Drive column … Refer to Figs. 2-17 and 2-18. 
The drive column raises and lowers the tunnel and cab. It is connected to the wheel carriage with trunnion pins, and

The vehicle direction detector and height detector are provided. The independent drive unit is provided at the end of

(1) Column assembly … Refer to Fig. 2-18. 
The column assembly co nsists of a top section and bottom section made of square steel pipe and ball screws, and 
slides between the top section and the bottom section. The top section moves up and down by  rotation of the ball 
screw. 
 
(2) Ball screw assembly … Refer to Fig. 2-18. 
The ball screw assembly consists of a set of ball screw, ball nut, beating mount, thrust bearing, etc. on each of the 
left and right sides. The upper end of the ball screw is directly connected to the vertical drive motor by way of the 
chain coupling.  The ball nut is connected to the joint block at the upper end of the bottom section. 
When each ball screw is rotated by the left and right vertical drive motors, the ball nut travels in the screw thread 
direction. By this mechanism, rotation of the screw is converted into linear motion of the nut.

The drive unit consists of a vertical drive motor, top frame supporting the motor, frame, sprockets and height 
detector. 
Manipulate the ball screw assembly within the upper and lower extension/retraction limit.

(1) Motor circuit … Refer to Fig. 2-28-1 
1) The three-phase AC power transmitted from the PDP enters the circuit breaker <MCCB1> for wiring on the 
control panel, and then supplied to the circuit breakers <MCCB2> <MCCB3> <MCCB4> for motor wiring by 
the magnetic contactor <MC> for power switching. 
2) The circuit breaker <MCCB2> is provided for left and  right closure drive motors and cab rotation drive motor. 
The circuit breaker <MCCB3> is provided for the raising/lowering motor, and the circuit breaker <MCCB4> is 
provided for the left and right horizontal travel drive motor. 
3) When the reversible magnetic  switch <RA1> is turned ON, the left closure moves up by the left closure drive 
motor <M1>. When the reversible magnetic switch <LW1> is turned ON, the left closure moves down. The 
motor <M1> is protected against burning caused by overload by the thermal relay <TH1>. 
In the same way, when the reversible magnetic switch <RA2> is turned ON, the right closure moves up. 
When the reversible magnetic switch <LW2> is turned ON, the right closure moves down.  The right closure 
drive motor <M2> is protected against burning caused by overload by the thermal relay <TH2>. 
4) When the reversible magnetic switch <L> is turned ON, the cab turns to the left by the cab rotation motor 
<M3>. When the reversible magnetic switch <R> is turned ON, the cab turns to the right. The motor <M3> 
is protected by the thermal relay <TH3>. 
5) The circuit breaker <MCCB3> for wiring is provided for the raising/lowering drive motor. 
When the reversible magnetic contactor <U> is turned ON while the magnetic switches <UDL> <UDR> are 
ON, the left and right raising/lowering motors <M11> <M12> rotate to move up the boarding bridge.  When 
the reversible magnetic contactor <D> is turned ON, the boarding bridge moves down.  
The motors <M11> <M12> are protected by the thermal relays <TH4L> <TH4R>. 
6) The circuit breaker <MCCB4> for wiring is provided for the left and right horizontal drive motors.  The 
power is supplied to the inverter <INV1> for left horizontal drive motor and the inverter <INV2> for right 
horizontal drive motor by way of the circuit breaker <MCCB4>. The inverters can arbitrarily control the 
number of rotations of the left and right horizontal drive motors <M5> <M6> that is directly related to the 
boarding bridge horizontal travel speed. The power received by the circuit breaker <MCCB4> is supplied to 
the left and right horizontal travel drive motor brakes <BR5> <BR6> by way of the magnetic contactor <BC>. 
When the magnetic contactor <BC> is turned ON, the power is supplied to the brakes <BR5> <BR6>.  As a 
result, the brakes are released, and the motors can rotate. When the magnetic contactor <BC> is turned OFF, 
the brakes become effective to stop travel of the boarding bridge.

10 Curtain Adjustment 
A) Adjustment of Cab Curtain 
To adjust the cab curtain tension, proceed with the following steps: 
1. Rotate the cab fully in the right-side direction to adjust the right-hand curtain, and opposite for the left hand contain. 
2. Open the top cover of the curtain barrels. 
3. On the top of the curtain barrels is a spring tensioned.  Using a wrench, maintain the spring tension and remove the 
locking pin. 
 
 
4. Turn the shaft counter-clockwise to increase the tension and clockwise to decrease the tension on the right hand barrel, 
and opposite for the left hand barrel. 
 
5. Replace the locking pin and rotate the cab in both directions. 
6. If the curtains wind and unwind smoothly, maintaining the required tension, close the top covers. 
 
B) Adjustment of Rotunda Curtain 
To adjust the rotunda curtain tension, proceed with the following steps: 
1. Rotate the “PAXWAY” fully in the right side direction to adjust the right hand curtain, and opposite for the left h a n d 
curtain. 
2. Open the hinged cover, which houses the two barrels. 
3. On the top of the curtain barrels is a spring tensioned. Using a wrench, maintain a spring tension and remove the 
locking pin. 
4. Turn the shaft clockwise to increase the tension and counter-clockwise to decrease the tension on the right hand barrel, 
and opposite for the left hand barrel. 
 
 
5. Replace the locking pin and rotate the rotunda in both directions. 
6. If the curtains wind and unwind smoothly, maintaining the required tension, close the hinged curtain covers.

11 Tunnel Roller Adjustment 
 
A) Tunnel A lower front roller 
Adjustment of this roller is not necessary because this roller is a datum point for adjusting the other rollers. 
 
B) Tunnel B lower rear roller 
(a) Fully retract the PBB. 
(b) Remove tunnel bottom and side access covers at locations of the rollers as well as weathering. 
(c) After loosening nuts, adjust vertical adjusting bolts so that the clearances between tunnel A and B floors at the 
front and rear ends of the tunnels become equal. 
(d) Adjust horizontal adjusting bolts so that left and right clearance between tunnel A and B side walls become 
equal. Loosen then the bolts by 1/2 turn to give the rollers smooth Contact with rails. 
(e) After completing the adjustment, tighten the nuts firmly. 
(f) Attach tunnel bottom and side access covers and weathering. 
 
C) Tunnel B upper front roller 
(a) Fully retract the PBB. 
(b) Remove tunnel roof and side access covers at locations of the rollers. 
(c) After loosening nuts, adjust vertical and horizontal adjusting bolts so that the bolts are pressed to the rails and 
then loosen the bolts by 1/2 turn to give them smooth contact with the rails. 
(d) After completing the adjustment, tighten the nuts firmly. 
(e) Attach tunnel roof and side accesses covers. 
 
D) Tunnel B upper rear roller 
Remove access covers and weathering and then follow the same procedures of (3) above. 
 
E) Tunnel A side front roller 
(a) Fully extend the PBB. 
(b) Open tunnel floor ramp. 
(c) After loosening nuts, adjust bolts so that left and right tunnel B lower front rollers are pressed to rails equally. 
Loosen then the bolts by 1/2 turn to give the rollers smooth contact with rails. 
(d) After completing the adjustment, tighten the double nuts firmly. 
 
F) Tunnel alignment roller 
(a) Loosen double nuts of roller shaft. 
(b) Adjust roller shaft so that the rollers are pressed to tunnel rails. 
(c) Loosen the bolts by 1/2 turn and then tighten the double nuts firmly.

Keep tunnels level while adjusting rollers. 
Because of welded construction, tunnels are slightly deformed, twisted or bent. Nevertheless, rollers 
should be adjusted in such a manner that the corresponding pairs of rollers are equally in smooth 
contact with rails at any point of the PBB extension and retraction. 
Do not try to correct tunnel deformation by adjusting rollers. Tunnel deformation can by no means be 
corrected by adjusting rollers. 
After adjusting rollers, check rails for any foreign matters remaining on the tread which obstructs 
smooth running of the rollers. 
After completing adjustment of rollers, temporarily extend or retract the PBB several times to ensure

noises generate, then the rollers must be re-adjusted.
