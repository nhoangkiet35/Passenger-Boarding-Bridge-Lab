1: INTRODUCTION
This specification outlines the requirements for a potable water supply system to be mounted 
on a passenger boarding bridge (PBB) at airports. The system is designed to provide safe, clean 
drinking water for passengers and crew.
2: SCOPE
The potable water supply system will include all necessary components to ensure the delivery of 
potable water, including piping, fittings, valves, filtration systems, and connections to the airport’s 
water supply.
3: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
The system must comply with all local, state, and federal regulations regarding potable water 
supply.
All materials must be certified for drinking water use.
4: SYSTEM COMPONENTS
4.1: Piping:
1. Material: Stainless Steel grade 316 to ISO 10312:2008 and AS/NZS4020:1999 (for potable water)..
2. Size: Minimum diameter of 25mm to ensure adequate flow rate.
4.2: Valves:
1. Type: Includes shut-off valves and pressure-regulating valves.
2. Material: Stainless Steel or Lead free Brass.
3. Backflow prevention (RPZ) to prevent contamination of the potable water supply.
4. Systems shall be designed to 500KPa supply pressure and maximum flow rate 100 litres per 
minute.
4.3: Filtration System:
1. Includes a 50 micron filtration system to remove particulates, chlorine, and other 
contaminants.
2. Regular maintenance and filter replacement schedule should be established.
4.4: Connections:
1. 1" BSP Male connection exterior to reel enclosure to accept airport water supply.
2. 400V from airport supply to electrical cabinet.
3. Data connection point inside enclosure for connection to flow sensor (IO link)
4.5: Operation:
Manual pull to extend, motor driven retraction vis deadman push button.

5: ACCESSIBILITY AND MOUNTING
The system should be designed for easy access for maintenance and inspection.
Mounting brackets and supports must be designed to securely attach the system to the boarding 
bridge without compromising structural integrity.
6: USER INTERFACE
Simple and easily understood operation and function.
Clear signage indicating the source and potability of the water must be displayed.
7: MAINTENANCE AND SERVICEABILITY
Detailed maintenance manual, including schedules for inspections, cleaning, and replacement of 
parts shall be provided.
The system should have easy access points for service and maintenance.
8: INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS
Installation must be carried out by certified personnel following all safety regulations.
System should undergo pressure testing and quality assurance checks upon installation.
9: DOCUMENTATION
Complete documentation, including installation guidelines, and maintenance manuals shall be 
provided.
10: WARRANTY
The system includes 2 year parts warranty covering parts from the date of supply.
